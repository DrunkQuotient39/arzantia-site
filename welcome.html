    Open your table

    Arzantia runs on your computer. Pick how you'd like to start, then come back here when you're ready.

    In your browser Enter the table

    Your table lives online. Open your campaigns, choose this one, and press Enter the table.

    1. Open your campaigns
    2. Choose your campaign
    3. Press Enter the table
    Open your campaigns
    Game night Bring the room in

    Put the map on a TV with a second browser window, and let players scan the join code with their phones.

    1. Open the TV view on the big screen
    2. Players scan the code
    3. Each picks a character
    On game night Scan, pick, play

    When your DM opens the table, scan the code on their screen or TV, or press Enter the table from your campaigns.

    1. Open the code or link on your phone
    2. Choose your character
    3. Your phone is now your character sheet
    Good to know Any phone works

    No app to install. If you switch phones mid-session, sign in and take your character back.

    The quick tour

    One table, three screens

    Here's who sees what once you're playing.

    The DM's screen

    Scenes in the side bar, tokens on the map, and Combat beside it. Everything has undo.

    Players' phones

    Each player's character, HP and turn. Their moves reach your map at once.

    The TV

    Open the table with ?tv=1 on the TV to show the map, the turn order and party health.
