Run your own campaign

Your campaign lives on your PC. On that PC, double-click Arcane.cmd in the Arcane folder: it sets itself up and opens a page where you sign in once with this account. Your campaign then shows up here, and you invite your players from its DM tools. After that, double-click Arcane.cmd before each session.
