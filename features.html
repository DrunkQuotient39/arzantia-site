Maps & scenes

Design worlds worth exploring

Every scene keeps its map, its tokens, its fog and its notes, so switching from the tavern to the crypt takes one click.

  • A scene library with previews, in a list or a grid, that you can resize and rename
  • Set a map's real size in metres. Tokens keep their spots on the picture
  • Turn a map a quarter at a time, for everyone at once, with undo
  • Paint fog away by hand, and lift a scene into the 3D terrain view

Movement & vision

Every step measured, every room earned

Drag a token and the ruler counts the metres as you go. Each token sees as far as its vision, and what it sees comes out of the fog for everyone.

  • A 1.5 m grid and a ruler in metres
  • Vision per token, from 0 to 15 m
  • The TV and the phones see only what's been cleared

Tokens & conditions

Bring every character to life

Characters and monsters share one sheet: portrait, HP, defence, speed, level and stats, shown in the token's card when you select it.

  • Conditions glow on the token in their own colour, with rounds left on the badge
  • Characters and the bestiary show as a list or a grid of every token
  • Select a group and change them together: HP, conditions, visibility
  • The card waits a moment after a drag, so it never gets in the way

    Dice

    Dice with weight to them

    The dice in Arzantia tumble in 3D with real physics, and the face that lands is the result. Press R on the map and throw.

    • d4 to d20, up to four dice and modifiers in one throw
    • Relaxed, Steady or Quick throws, with sounds from real contacts
    • Saved rolls for the attacks you make every turn

    Tap the die to roll a d20

    Phones & TV

    The whole room, in the game

    Players join by scanning the code on the TV or opening your invite link. Their phone becomes their character, and the TV shows the battle to everyone.

    • See who's at the table from the "online" chip in the header
    • The TV shows the map, the turn order and party health, never what's hidden
    • Shared rolls on the TV, and secret rolls kept secret

    Map maker

    A fresh map before anyone notices

    Pick a place, set how busy it is, and Arzantia draws it on the grid in seconds. Same seed, same map, every time.

    • Dungeons, caves, forests, taverns and ruins
    • The grid is exact, so tokens and the ruler line up
    • Torches, hearths and campfires light the scene

    Marks on the map

    Paint the battlefield

    Fire, frost, poison gas, a wall of lightning. Lay a spell's area on the map in its true shape and size, and see at once who's caught in it.

    • Thirteen marks, each with its own look and motion
    • Circles, cones, walls and squares, measured in metres
    • A cone is as wide at its end as it is long, as the rules say
