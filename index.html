        Questions

        Before you roll

        What do I need to run Arzantia?

        A browser and an account. Arzantia is a website: sign in, open your campaign, and your table is there. There's nothing to download or install.

        Do my players need to install anything?

        No. Players open the table in their phone's browser by scanning the code or following your invite link, then choose their character.

        Can we play online?

        Yes. Your table lives online: send your invite link and players join from anywhere, on a phone, a tablet or a laptop.

        Where are my campaigns saved?

        With your table, so every scene, token and note is waiting next session. Your Arzantia account keeps your campaigns, your members and your invites, so your party can always find the table.

        Does it work on Mac, Windows or a tablet?

        Yes. Arzantia runs in any modern browser, on any computer, tablet or phone.

        Why is it called Arzantia?

        Arz is the Arabic word for cedar, the tree of Lebanon: the tree of the oldest quest, of the ships that carried the alphabet, and of a grove that has stood for a thousand years. Arzantia is the land of the cedar, a place where stories take root. Read the story
